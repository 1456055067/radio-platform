# What is Radio Platform?

The Uniden **SDS200** is a desktop radio scanner. It listens to public
safety, aviation, weather and other radio traffic and shows what it hears
on its front-panel display. It also has a network port, which lets other
equipment control it and receive its audio.

Radio Platform uses that network port to bring many scanners, in many
places, into one web page.

## What you can do with it

- **See every scanner at a glance.** A dashboard shows each scanner's
  state: what system and channel it is on, signal strength, and whether
  it is online.
- **Operate a scanner remotely.** A "virtual scanner" in the browser
  mirrors the front panel. Authorised people can change volume and
  squelch, hold on a channel, or skip one.
- **Listen live.** If audio is switched on for a scanner, people with
  permission can listen to it in the browser.
- **Keep a record.** Transmissions can be logged and, if switched on,
  recorded. Recordings can be turned into searchable text transcripts.
- **Look back.** Search past transmissions, review events, and see trends
  in an analytics view.
- **Stay accountable.** Every action people take is written to an audit
  log that is designed to show if anyone tampers with it.

Audio, recording and transcription are **off unless an administrator
switches them on**, scanner by scanner.

## Who it is for

| You are… | You will mostly use… |
|---|---|
| A **viewer** (for example a supervisor or analyst) | The dashboard, events and transmission history |
| An **operator** | The virtual scanner to listen to and control scanners |
| An **administrator** | Site and scanner set-up, users and roles, audio and retention settings |

The [Using the console](using-the-console.md) page explains each of these.

## What it is not

- It does **not** decrypt encrypted radio traffic, and never will.
- It does not replace a licensed dispatch or recording system.
- It is not yet proven on real hardware. See [Project status](project-status.md).
